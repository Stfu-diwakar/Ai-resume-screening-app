Role: Business Analyst

Overview:
We are looking for a Business Analyst who can translate business needs into clear requirements and actionable insights. The candidate will work with business stakeholders, product teams, and technical teams.

Required Skills:
- SQL and advanced Excel
- Requirement gathering and documentation
- Data analysis and problem solving
- Business process mapping
- Communication and stakeholder management

Preferred Skills:
- Power BI or Tableau
- Python and Pandas
- Jira and Agile methodologies
- BRD, FRD, and user stories

Experience:
0-3 years of relevant experience.

Responsibilities:
- Gather and document business requirements.
- Analyze processes and identify improvement opportunities.
- Build dashboards and communicate KPI trends.
- Coordinate with developers and QA teams.
