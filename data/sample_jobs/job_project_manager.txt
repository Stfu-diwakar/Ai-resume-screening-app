Role: Project Manager

Overview:
We are seeking a Project Manager to coordinate cross-functional delivery, manage project schedules, and keep stakeholders aligned.

Required Skills:
- Project planning and execution
- Agile and Scrum methodologies
- Risk and issue management
- Stakeholder communication
- Jira or equivalent project tracking tools
- Resource and timeline coordination

Preferred Skills:
- PMP or CAPM certification
- Confluence
- Budget tracking and reporting
- Software development lifecycle knowledge

Experience:
2-5 years of project coordination or project management experience.

Responsibilities:
- Define project milestones, deliverables, and schedules.
- Track dependencies, risks, blockers, and progress.
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies and status meetings.
- Prepare project reports and communicate changes.
